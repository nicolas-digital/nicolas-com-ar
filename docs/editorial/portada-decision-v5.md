## Prompt final

Use case: photorealistic-natural / editorial cover.
Asset: hyperrealistic wide 16:9 photograph for an executive article and impactful social card.

ARTICLE BRIEF: The article asks how to connect business strategy, data and AI to decisions a business needs to make. Its thesis is to choose one concrete decision, establish the evidence, decide what AI may recommend or execute, and retain accountable human judgment. It discusses choosing a bounded pilot and deciding whether to continue, correct or stop. Warehouses and replenishment are merely examples, NOT the subject. The image must evoke considered executive judgment, not technology training, a warehouse supervisor, a consumer or a person posing with a device.

NEW VISUAL IDEA: "The moment a recommendation becomes a decision."
Scene: a very close, intimate, oblique overhead editorial photograph of one executive's actual working surface. No face, no room, no meeting. A real human hand with natural mature skin texture holds a graphite pencil and is decisively circling a short handwritten choice on a cream paper decision brief. The paper is the main physical focal point. A thin modern laptop just beyond the paper is visibly showing an AI assistant's recommendation, with a restrained text-based interface rather than charts or a presentation. A second partially visible sheet supplies a simple evidence comparison. Everything belongs to ONE initiative; no collage or floating layers.
The physical decision brief has only these short, readable Spanish lines, with ample whitespace:
"DECISIÓN DE NEGOCIO"
"Probar antes de escalar"
"Alcance acotado"
"Responsable definido"
Near bottom three discrete choices printed in a row: "PILOTO" "ESCALAR" "DETENER".
The graphite pencil is circling ONLY "PILOTO", a real slightly imperfect pencil mark.
Laptop in upper background, partially cropped but intelligible: a minimal interface with the heading "Análisis con IA", then two short alternative blocks "Opción A" and "Opción B". No fabricated metrics or dense microtext. The laptop is visibly secondary and in modestly softer focus.
The evidence sheet, partially under the decision brief, has only the heading "Evidencia" and three short row labels "Datos" "Costos" "Riesgos". No charts, decorative grids, arrows, invented logos, random numbers, post-its, coffee mug or surplus props.

Composition and photography: photograph from about 45 degrees above, tight crop, tactile paper and purposeful hand occupy the center-right 60%, laptop recedes toward the upper right, leftmost 25% is quiet dark natural walnut tabletop for a later short social-card headline. Main action must still read when reduced to a thumbnail. Warm directional natural window light makes the pencil, paper edge and hand exceptionally tangible; deep but plausible shadows. Cream paper, charcoal laptop, muted forest-green small interface accents. Full-frame 50mm editorial lens, realistic perspective, natural grain, believable hand anatomy. Premium contemporary business magazine photo, authentic rather than polished stock photo, no theatrical sci-fi effects. The tension is a machine offering alternatives while a person explicitly chooses a responsible next step. Do not attempt to explain the entire article.

Constraints: no meeting, presentation, warehouse, store, shelves, tablet-holder portrait, miniature architecture, bonsai, robots, brains, glowing lines, holograms, chess pieces, handshakes, dashboards full of bar charts, MIT branding, fake client identity, giant title overlay or watermark. Verbatim Spanish labels, no additional text. This is a fictional illustrative scene, not a real company document. Generate a local image file.
